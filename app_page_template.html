The Last Line

You're the last remaining member of a human outpost just outside our solar system. You were out with a new experimental prototype fighter when the outpost was overrun by an unknown enemy. Now it is just you and this one prototype standing in the way of the alien threat. There is no backup coming, you cannot send a message to earth on your short range radio. YOU ARE THE LAST LINE OF DEFENCE!
This game was created in the spirit of the classic endless space shooters. Thank you for playing, we hope you enjoy it as much as we do.
