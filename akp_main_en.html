Easy Pre-School Basics

This small app aims to help preschool children 2-5 years old learn and improve their skills with colors, shapes, number recognition and counting.
The activities have specifically been made simple to facilitate playing unassisted without the interference of adverts and accidentally exiting the open activity. While the main screen does contain ads to help pay for the app development, the activities do not have any ads. You may also buy the paid version of this app at https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=za.co.lifestoriesdevelopement.kinderkleure.english.paid&hl=en&gl=US for a completely ad free playing experience.
The activities use a special mechanism to exit so that your little one cannot accidentally exit the activity by pressing the back or recent apps button.
The graphics used are also similar to hand drawn crayon pictures that should appeal to your children.
Thank you for installing the app and please remember to rate and comment on the Google Play store. I will accept all comments and suggestions to help improve this for all our children.
